    Where these numbers come from

    • Neighbors and meaning profile: the nine word2vec models in v_2_word2vec_models (three periods, three random seeds each; 300 dimensions, skip-gram). Similarity is cosine similarity, averaged over the seeds. Neighbors rank by that average.
    • Word groups: bodily / bacterial = bacterial, bacteria, disease, decay, decomposition, infection, putrid. crowding / population = overpopulation, overcrowding, population explosion, overpopulated. industrial / chemical = chemical, industrial, smog, sewage, factories, fumes. nuclear / radiation = radioactivity, fallout, radiation, radioactive, nuclear, atomic. I chose these lists; changing them changes the lines.
    • Frequency: counts from each model's vocabulary, per million tokens. Each period was subsampled to the same size (about 54 million tokens) before training, and frequent word pairs such as "nuclear war" were merged into one token first, so counts can run slightly below raw text counts. For the words in out_w2v_v2/tables/freq.csv the figures are identical to that file.
    • Books: novel_keyness_top_by_word.csv, Dunning G2 of each volume against the rest of the corpus (minimum 3 uses, top 15 per word kept). It covers the 101 environmental seed words. Volumes that bind several novels under one HathiTrust ID show their titles joined with a slash.
    • Periods: 1945–61 (before Silent Spring), 1962–71, 1972–80 (Clean Water Act onward).
